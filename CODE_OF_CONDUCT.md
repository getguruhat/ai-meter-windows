# Code of conduct

AI Meter is a small project, and everyone who takes part should feel welcome: people asking questions, reporting bugs, reviewing or writing code.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific and useful.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam and self-promotion that has nothing to do with the discussion.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, email hello@guruhat.com. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies in this repo's issues, pull requests and discussions. It's adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
